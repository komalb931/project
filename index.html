The Idea

A haven for recreation,
relaxation and wellness

Baramati Club offers an exquisite haven for recreation, relaxation and wellness, wrapped in beautiful architecture and interiors. Whether it's a leisurely day by the pool, an adrenaline-fuelled match, or a serene spa retreat, the Club is built to fulfil the desires of every visitor.

The Club is managed by Alta Hotels Pvt. Ltd. — a hospitality name that has grown from a single restaurant established in 1956 into a group of 25+ units spanning hotels, banqueting, bulk catering and specialised foods.

Facilities · Amenities

Everything under one roof

Mouthwatering Food

Our Restaurant

A fine-dining restaurant for a romantic dinner, a celebration, or a relaxed gathering — from mouthwatering appetizers to sumptuous main courses and desserts. The poolside lounge is perfect for light bites and refreshing beverages.

Sip the Joy

Our Bar

A dedicated bar where skilled bartenders craft exquisite cocktails to suit your mood — a classic concoction or a unique creation, always tailor-made.
